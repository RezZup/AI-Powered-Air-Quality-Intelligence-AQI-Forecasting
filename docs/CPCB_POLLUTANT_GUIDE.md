# Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) Standard Guide

## 1. Primary Criteria Pollutants

| Pollutant | Averaging Period | Good (0-50) | Satisfactory (51-100) | Moderate (101-200) | Poor (201-300) | Very Poor (301-400) | Severe (401-500) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **PM2.5** (µg/m³) | 24-hr | 0–30 | 31–60 | 61–90 | 91–120 | 121–250 | 250+ |
| **PM10** (µg/m³) | 24-hr | 0–50 | 51–100 | 101–250 | 251–350 | 351–430 | 430+ |
| **NO2** (µg/m³) | 24-hr | 0–40 | 41–80 | 81–180 | 181–280 | 281–400 | 400+ |
| **NH3** (µg/m³) | 24-hr | 0–200 | 201–400 | 401–800 | 801–1200 | 1201–1800 | 1800+ |
| **SO2** (µg/m³) | 24-hr | 0–40 | 41–80 | 81–380 | 381–800 | 801–1600 | 1600+ |
| **CO** (mg/m³) | 8-hr | 0–1.0 | 1.1–2.0 | 2.1–10 | 10.1–17 | 17.1–34 | 34+ |
| **Ozone** (µg/m³) | 8-hr | 0–50 | 51–100 | 101–168 | 169–208 | 209–748 | 748+ |

## 2. Health Advisory Implications

- **Good / Satisfactory:** Minimal impact on public health.
- **Moderate:** Breathing discomfort to people with lungs, asthma, and heart diseases.
- **Poor:** Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure.
- **Very Poor / Severe:** Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure; significant effect on healthy individuals.
